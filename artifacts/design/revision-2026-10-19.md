## Argument and subject inventory

Relevo means a relay replacement; one job retains its identity; attempts have
distinct owners/tokens; PostgreSQL commits acceptance/history together; independent
recovery acts on expiry; recorded stale reports return 409; three attempts are the
default budget; actual crash capture has two attempts; effect protection has its
own transaction; retained benchmarks include regressions; local Compose is usable
without paid hosting. These facts, not invented customer proof, supply the page.

Offer → illustrate a handoff → explain responsibilities once → inspect the actual
job/attempt record → inspect test/measurement proof and limits → answer questions
→ run locally. Each section has one question and one visual job.

Taste dealt dense-tiles, quiet-instrument and product-performs. Selected
**product-performs**, seed **40997419**, because a readable working handoff
fragment carries this product's identity better than a board of tiles or a narrow
small-text instrument. Palette and type were kept. The proof-logo and quiet-line
footer slots are replaced because there are no customers and the user explicitly
requires a grouped schematic footer. Drawn software/real evidence replace stock
architecture photographs; no four-image quota or entrance-animation quota will
override the user's request to remove repetition and clutter.

Idea: On this page, the job that survives a change of owner becomes the persistent
object connecting the illustration to its real recorded proof.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One job stays; its owner changes. Each section answers a different question,
with a single exploded-view moment rather than a repeated diagram.

OWN-WORLD: Keep Relevo's chalk, graphite and burnt orange and existing self-hosted
fonts. Use larger reading text, fewer rules, fewer technical labels, and flat
filled interface fragments. Original vectors remain logical illustrations.

STORY: Understand the offer, operate an illustrative handoff, inspect architecture,
read one actual recovery record, evaluate evidence/limits, then run locally.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two-line headline and brief explanation on the left; one readable
interactive handoff stage on the right. One Engine button and a quiet Docs link.
A crisp graphite ASCII band closes the hero. Phone composition preserves the
headline, action and readable handoff controls.

FORM: Code-led, user-pinned Griffin/Cierto/Etched reference roles, informed by Taste
product-performs seed 40997419. Home owns the only exploded system. Engine Flow
uses a flat request path; Replay uses an attempt history. Compact component
selection replaces the long scroll. FAQ expands smoothly with native buttons,
proper regions, rapid reversal, and reduced-motion fallback.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Sections and acceptance

- Hero: understandable offer + illustrative handoff; one primary Engine action.
- ASCII: clearly visible glyph field on graphite; bounded drawing and pause modes.
- Architecture: one exploded view, compact responsibilities, readable legend.
- Recovery: replace the rejected three-column lifecycle with a continuous actual
  record. Distinguish job identity, two attempts, events, and untimed interventions.
- Evidence/limits: dated tests and measured results; external effects can repeat.
- FAQ: smooth open/close, one answer at a time, focus and reduced motion intact.
- Closing/footer: Docs action, original schematic, grouped navigation and utilities.
- Engine: preserve all replay/scenario/deep-link behavior; use distinct Flow and
  Replay visuals. No duplicate exploded art and no new privileged requests.
- Across pages: consistent action treatment; remove decorative arrow pairs.
