## Product truth

Relevo saves background work, assigns it to available workers, and makes another
attempt possible when an assignment expires and the retry budget permits it.
A retry starts the registered handler again; it does not resume its instructions.
Old owners cannot overwrite the newer executor state. Outside business actions
can still repeat and need their own protection. Built-in tasks are hashing, timed
work, and configured failures; reports/imports/media are illustrative integrations.
The public site explains sanitized recordings, not a live privileged console.
